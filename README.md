This application is a simple overlay to go over your predecessor game to allow you to see your custom builds on screen in game. The app is powered by omeda.city api. Your first startup will be slower, because the app downloads all the necessary images. You can click and drag the overlay anywhere on screen.

How to use:
1. Open the application, it will take a minute to load, as it extracts the necessary files, once loaded, select your hero.
2. Once you select a hero, click create new build.
3. Select your items and crest
4. Click Save
5. Now press select to collapse the display.
6. Click and drag the overlay anywhere you want.
7. PROFIT!!
